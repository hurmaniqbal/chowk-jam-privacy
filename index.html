Chowk Jam — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026
App: Chowk Jam for Android and iOS
Who is responsible for your data: Zealtouch AB, Sweden
Contact: abubakar.zealtouch@gmail.com

The short version

  • Chowk Jam has no accounts. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • Your game progress stays on your device.
  • The game shows ads and measures how it is played and when it crashes. To do that it includes software from Google (AdMob and Firebase), which collects some device and usage data described below.
  • Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before ads are loaded, and you can change your choice at any time.

What stays on your device only

The game saves the following in the app's local storage. It is not sent to us:

  • levels you have cleared, your stars and the highest level unlocked
  • your coin balance
  • your daily-challenge streak
  • whether sound is on
  • whether you have seen the "how to play" card
  • whether you have bought "Remove ads"

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, erases all of this. There is no cloud backup run by us and no sync between devices.

What is collected, by whom, and why

We do not run our own servers for this game. Data is collected by the following third-party software built into the app.

1. Google AdMob (advertising) and Google's consent tool

What it does: shows ads between some levels, and optional "reward" ads that you choose to watch for a hint, a continue or extra coins.

What it may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android advertising ID, or Apple's IDFA only if you allow tracking when iOS asks)
  • other device identifiers that are reset when you reinstall (for example an app set ID or vendor ID)
  • your IP address, which is also used to work out your general location (country / city level — never GPS)
  • device information: model, operating system, screen size, language, network type
  • which ads were shown and whether you tapped or watched them
  • diagnostic and performance information about the ads software
  • your consent choices

Why: to show ads, to choose which ads to show (personalised only where you have agreed, or where the law allows it without asking), to limit how often you see the same ad, to measure ad performance, and to detect fraud.

Shared with: Google and its advertising partners. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

2. Google Analytics for Firebase (how the game is played)

What it collects:

  • gameplay events we send: a level was started, cleared, failed or quit, with the level number, attempt number, lives left, vehicles left and seconds taken; a bump happened; a hint or continue was used; coins were earned or spent and why; an ad was shown or a reward was earned; "Remove ads" was bought (the product name only — no price, no payment details)
  • events Firebase records by itself: first open, session start, time in the app, app updates, and similar
  • a random app-instance ID, and device identifiers such as the advertising ID / app set ID (Android) or vendor ID (iOS)
  • device information: model, operating system version, app version, language, and country / region worked out from your IP address (the IP address itself is not stored by Analytics)

We do not send your name, email or anything you type, and we do not set a user ID.

Why: to understand which levels are too hard or too easy, how features are used, and to improve the game.

Shared with: Google (Firebase). See Firebase privacy and security.

3. Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports)

What it collects when the app crashes or hits a serious error: the error and stack trace, the state of the app at the time, device model, operating system version, orientation, free memory and disk, whether the device is jailbroken/rooted, the time of the crash, and a random installation ID that lets crashes from one install be grouped.

Why: to find and fix bugs.

Shared with: Google (Firebase).

4. In-app purchase (Google Play Billing / Apple App Store)

"Remove ads" is bought through your Google Play or Apple account. The payment is handled entirely by Google or Apple; we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns from the store whether the purchase succeeded, and remembers that on your device. Google's and Apple's own privacy policies apply to the purchase.

What we do not collect

No account or profile; no name, email address or phone number; no contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone; no precise (GPS) location; no health, financial or messaging data. The app asks for no such permissions.

Consent and your choices

  • Consent prompt. In regions where consent is required (for example the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent form the first time it starts. Ads are not requested until you have made a choice.
  • Changing your mind. Where a privacy choice applies to you, the home screen shows an "Ad privacy choices" link. Tap it to reopen the form and change or withdraw your consent at any time.
  • iOS tracking permission. If iOS asks whether to allow the app to track you, you can refuse; ads still work but are less tailored. Change this later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads. iOS: see above).
  • Fewer ads. The one-time "Remove ads" purchase stops the ads shown between levels. Optional reward ads are only played when you ask for them.
  • Stop everything. Uninstalling the app stops all collection and erases the data on your device.

The app has no in-app switch to turn off analytics or crash reporting. To stop them, uninstall the app.

Children

Chowk Jam is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, contact us at abubakar.zealtouch@gmail.com and we will take steps to have it deleted.

How long data is kept

  • On your device: until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Firebase Analytics: data tied to an identifier is kept for up to 14 months. Aggregated statistics that do not identify a device may be kept longer.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports are kept for 90 days.
  • AdMob: kept by Google under its own retention policies (see Google's Privacy Policy).

Where data is processed

Google may process data on servers in the United States and other countries.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to it, and to complain to your local data-protection authority. Because the game has no accounts, we usually cannot identify you from the data Google holds; to help us act on a request, contact us at abubakar.zealtouch@gmail.com. You can also use the controls above and Google's own tools.
